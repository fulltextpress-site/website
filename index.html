2084

We Have Migrated

Samuel Ewan Reade

Nothing is forbidden. Words are simply replaced.

At 03:26, while she was still under anaesthetic, Ivy Holt agreed to give up her child. The record says so.

Guardian: process-side proxy acceptance.

London, 2084. The city is clean, warm and quiet, and almost nothing is said in full. Every announcement comes in the same calm woman’s voice, and it always pauses for breath before the last two words.

2084: We Have Migrated is a future history told through its own records, and a philosophical dystopia about language, consent, and who gets to say ‘we’.
